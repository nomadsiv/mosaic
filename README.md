# mosaic
A personal intelligence system that brings together a lifetime of digital archives. Powered by Tara, a conversational agent for remembering, discovering, correlating, reflecting and making meaning of one's life.

_A life in fragments. A whole in conversation._

A personal intelligence system grounded in the digital record of a life — its writings, correspondence, photographs, documents, creations and everyday experiences.

At its heart is Tara, a conversational agent designed to help me remember, discover, correlate, reflect and make meaning from my ever-growing personal archive.
